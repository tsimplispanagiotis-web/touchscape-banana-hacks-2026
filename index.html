VISUAL VECTOR DENSITY —

Measuring output paths and visual complexity. Not tactile usability.

◈
Privacy by design

The vision models execute on your device. Uploaded photos never leave the browser; model weights are fetched from Hugging Face.

ⓘ

Experimental vector draft — not validated tactile graphics. Depth is relative, segmentation can misidentify objects, and true tactile production needs specialist review, sizing, and user testing. The Unicode braille legend is a visual representation, not an embossing instruction.

THE PIPELINE

More than a pretty filter.

Two neural networks feed a deterministic geometry engine. Every contour in your output comes from image-derived structure, not decorative effects.

WHY IT MATTERS

Art shouldn't stop at sight.

Photographs, learning materials, and personal memories are overwhelmingly visual. Tactile diagrams are often expensive and labor-intensive to prepare. Touchscape explores a new first step: let AI generate an editable tactile draft, then let people refine it together.
